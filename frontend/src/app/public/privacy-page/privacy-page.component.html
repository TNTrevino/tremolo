Who we are

Tremolo is a music-practice website at tremolonotes.com for students in about 6th through 12th grade. This policy explains in plain language what the site stores, why, who can see it, and how to remove it. If a sentence here does not match what the software does, the sentence is wrong and we will fix it.

What we collect

  • Account — your first and last name, email address, whether you're a student or a teacher, and a password stored only as a one-way bcrypt hash (or, for Google sign-in, a Google identifier and no password at all).
  • Grade level, optionally — 6th through 12th grade, or "other." That's a grade, not a birthday: the least specific age information useful to us.
  • Practice records for every finished game — which game, how many questions were asked and answered correctly, notes-per-minute, how long the session took, the date and time, and the assignment it was linked to, if any.
  • Settings — your per-game configuration and keyboard bindings.
  • Classes — for a student, which classes you've joined; for a teacher, the classes, rosters, and assignments you created.
  • Friends — the connections you add.
  • Security housekeeping — a count of recent failed sign-in attempts and whether the account is temporarily locked.
  • Email we send you — the message itself, the address it went to, and its delivery status, so we can tell you what happened if it never arrives.
  • Server logs — request logs kept on our own machine. Some sign-in log lines contain the email address used.

Two database columns, "instrument" and "school", exist from an earlier design. No screen fills them in today, and we do not ask for either.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking scripts.
  • No advertising cookies.
  • No payment information.
  • No photos, microphone, or camera access.
  • No precise location.
  • No student ID numbers or Social Security numbers.
  • No disciplinary records, health records, or school grades.
  • No free-form profile text.

Why we collect it

  • Signing you in, and keeping you signed in.
  • Showing you your own progress.
  • Letting a teacher whose class you joined see that class.
  • Sending account email, such as a password reset.
  • Keeping accounts from being broken into.

That is the whole list.

We do not sell your information, and we do not advertise

We do not sell, rent, or trade student information. We do not show ads. We do not build advertising profiles. We do not use student information to train advertising or recommendation systems.

Who can see what

  • You — everything on your own account.
  • A teacher — for a student who joined that teacher's active class: the student's name, practice statistics, and attempts at that teacher's assignments. Not the student's email, settings, keyboard bindings, friends, or other teachers' classes. Access ends when the class is archived or the student leaves it.
  • Other students — only what a friend connection shows.
  • We — the person who runs Tremolo can reach the database to fix and back up the service, and looks at individual accounts only to fix a problem or answer a request.

Nobody else sees your information — except that outbound email is handed to Google's Gmail servers for delivery, the way any email has to be handed to a mail provider.

Where your information lives

On computers we own and operate ourselves. The database is PostgreSQL and is not reachable from the internet. Traffic is encrypted with HTTPS. Signed-in browsers hold a sign-in token in local storage; signing out removes it.

Email

Account purposes only: password resets, address verification, account notices. No newsletters, no marketing, nothing to unsubscribe from.

How long we keep it

Account and practice history stay until the account is deleted. Account deletion is being added to the Account page now; until it ships, email us and we delete everything on request. Deletion removes the account, practice history, settings, bindings, class memberships, friend connections, and pending email to the address, permanently.

If a teacher deletes their account, their classes and assignments go with it; students keep their own practice history. Server logs rotate on the machine.

Students under 13 (COPPA)

Some users are under 13. COPPA requires verifiable parental consent, and the FTC recognizes that a school may consent on a parent's behalf when a service is educational-use-only and collects no more than the purpose needs. That is Tremolo's posture: a student under 13 should reach Tremolo through a teacher, with a class join code, under the school's authorization. We collect only what a practice tool needs, use it only for practice, show it only to the student and their own teacher, sell nothing, and show no ads. We do not knowingly collect information from a child under 13 outside a school arrangement.

Parents: email us and we delete the account and everything in it.

Schools and FERPA

School use makes practice records part of the student's education records. We act as a "school official" with a "legitimate educational interest" under FERPA (34 CFR § 99.31(a)(1)): the school stays in control, we use data only to provide the service, and we re-disclose nothing. Schools can direct hand-over or deletion at any time.

A district that needs a signed data-processing agreement should ask — we have a template ready.

Your choices

  • See your data — a data-export button is being added to the Account page. Until it ships, email us and we send your complete records.
  • Delete your data — same story: the Account page is gaining this too, and until it does, email us.
  • Correct your data — email us.
  • Parents and schools may make any of these requests on a student's behalf.

Changes to this policy
